# 02 · Decisions and numbers

Step 2. This is the single source of truth for every decision, rule, number, price, threshold, percentage, duration and named role in "Certified Practitioner — response to Kitestring draft 3". Later steps refer to rows here by number (D1, D2, …) and do not repeat the values. Prepared 2026-09-30.

**How to read it.**

- **Where** gives the section of the response and its line numbers (L…) in the file as it is in the folder. "Thread" means Javeline's answers in this project, recorded in 01-inventory.md §4.
- **Status** follows how the response itself words each item. It says nothing about whether Mujo agrees.
  - **DECIDED**: the response states it as settled.
  - **PROPOSAL**: the response calls it a starting value, target, estimate ("about"), or something it "would" do. L351 puts the flow-funding experiment, the founding oligarchy and the levy rates in this group ("starting values, not decisions"). L194 does the same for the Test Card targets.
  - **OPEN**: the response leaves it open, or gives no value, or states it two ways. The owner is named in brackets. Where the response names "Jeremy", the owner is Javeline (Jeremy is Javeline's pseudonym).
  - **ASSUMPTION (needs clarification)**: rests on a source that is not in the folder, as Javeline asked on 2026-09-30.
- **C1, C2, …** point to the conflicts listed after the table. Both sides are shown there, and nothing has been picked.
