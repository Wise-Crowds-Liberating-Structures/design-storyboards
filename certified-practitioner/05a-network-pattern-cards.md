### 1. Context of practice: label each session

**DECIDED 2026-10-06 (Javeline, D198).**


The app draws the session's own web from its record. Each co-signer is a dot coloured by user group, and each line is "practised together". It then names the closest card.
- A session where all players come from one group is **Birds of a Feather**.
- A session that mixes groups is **Celebrating Diversity**.
- A session where one person is the only link between two groups is **Bridging the Gaps**.

This is computed from counted evidence only (D182). It is shown on the session record, like the session's tier (D42).

Suggested next move, shown with the label (PROPOSAL):

| Session card | Suggested move |
|---|---|
| Birds of a Feather | Invite someone from another group to your next session |
| Celebrating Diversity | Keep this mix: offer the same people a follow-up session |
| Bridging the Gaps | Ask the bridging person to introduce the two groups directly, so the link doesn't rest on one person |

### 2. Position of a person: label where someone sits in the web

**DECIDED 2026-10-06 (Javeline, D198).**

Over a season, from all their co-signed sessions, the app shows the card that best fits a person's position:
- **Bridging the Gaps (Boundary Spanner):** links groups that otherwise don't meet.
- **Flat Hierarchy (Hub & Spoke):** many people practise *with them*, but not with each other.
- **The Inner Circle:** part of a tight core.
- **Periphery:** linked to the core through one or two people.
- **New Kids on the Block** (card: Loneliest Number): practises alone or only once.

This is shown on the profile and in the certificate's evidence profile (D160, D162). It describes the network, not the person, in the same spirit as OGD Eval, which "evaluates the practice, never the person" (D113).

Suggested next move, shown with the label (PROPOSAL):

| Position | Suggested move |
|---|---|
| Bridging the Gaps | Introduce two people from different groups to each other |
| Flat Hierarchy | Let one of the people around you host a session, so they practise with each other |
| The Inner Circle | Pair up with someone on the edge of the web |
| Periphery | Join a session hosted by someone you haven't practised with yet |
| New Kids on the Block | See open sessions and hosts nearby to practise with |
| Gatekeeper | Introduce the two sides you link, so they can meet without you |

Gatekeeper is listed for when a person is the only link between two sides (the Here Be Dragons card, profile name DECIDED in guardrail 4).

### 3. Season reflection: what do we want more of?

**DECIDED 2026-10-06 (Javeline, D198):** at each equinox gathering, a group looks back on the season through its own webs. These are the session cards (use 1) and the positions (use 2) computed from that season's co-signed records. The group reflects on **which patterns and which people it wants more of** next season.

Social Network Webbing is the natural structure for this. Its steps 6 and 7 ("who isn't but should be?", "how to get these nerds to talk?") feed directly into inviting new communities, which already belongs to the equinox conversations (D136).

The "say / actually / should" layering is dropped (Javeline, 2026-10-06).

## Guardrails

1. **Descriptive, never paid.** No card position moves money or the ladder. Payment follows use only (D168). If being a Boundary Spanner paid, people would fake bridges.
2. **Before the trust wire, only within the pilot community** (D157, D173). Positions that span other communities are shown per community, labelled "not yet cross-verified".
3. **Opt in to being shown** (D94). A person can hide their position card. Session context cards stay on the record, because they describe the session, not one person.
4. **The labels are mirrors, not verdicts.** **DECIDED 2026-10-06 (Javeline, D198):** on profiles, "Loneliest Number" is shown as **New Kids on the Block**, and "Here Be Dragons" is shown by its network term, **Gatekeeper**.
