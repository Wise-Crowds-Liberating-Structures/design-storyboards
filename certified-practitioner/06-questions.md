**How to read it**
- **Answer** is Javeline's answer where one exists. "Open" means there isn't one yet.
- **Who decides** is always named:
  - **Javeline** (Jeremy is Javeline's pseudonym).
  - **Mujo** (NAOMS).
  - **The pilot group**, confirming starting values (D170).
  - **The commons**, meaning user groups deciding together (D166).
- **Blocks** has these values:
  - **Pilot 1**: recording, reputation and record-based certificates (D158).
  - **Pilot 2**: wiki, online course and optional seal.
  - **Flow start**: the equinox vote that starts flow funding (D165, D169).
  - **Later**: the trust wire, the end of the hatch, the marketplace or white label.
  - **No**: nothing waits on it.
- Every "Blocks" value is Claude's reading of the pilot plan (D158, D165): **PROPOSAL**, Javeline decides.
- Who-decides entries that Claude assigned are labelled PROPOSAL. All other owners come from the files.
