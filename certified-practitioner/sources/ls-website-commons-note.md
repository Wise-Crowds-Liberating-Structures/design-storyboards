# Source note · Liberating Structures website, LS Commons call for Stewards and Hosts

Text pasted by Javeline in the thread on 2026-10-06 as the LS website's note (answer to Q45). Copied verbatim below. No URL or date was given.

> We are setting the stage for a generative social hub for the LS community to gather. To keep this ecosystem healthy, we are looking for Stewards and Hosts to help tend the forest. This isn't about central authority; it's about nurturing the soil so that new ideas can flourish and spread. To indicate your interest and/ or learn more about what hosting looks like in practice, please share your information via this form and someone will reach back out to you (Fisher Qua and Anna Jackson are helping to guide this development).
>
> Our goal is to ensure the LS Commons is a place where we can both remember our shared assets and celebrate new innovations. By participating, you help ensure that every riff and adaptation stays open and available for everyone, forever, under our shared Creative Commons CC BY-SA 4.0 License. We are currently preparing the space to: gather your hunches and field-tested prototypes; explore the remastered bedrock assets; and, connect with fellow practitioners. Let's see what we can grow together.
